01 · About Me

Hi, I’m Nick.

I’m interested in the point where scientific and technical problem-solving meets real human experience.

I am a student drawn to science, engineering, health, and the questions that connect them. I enjoy understanding how systems work—whether that means modeling a biological process, thinking through the risks behind an engineering decision, or asking how a technical solution changes when real people have to use it. I am especially interested in work that combines careful analysis with an awareness of the people and communities affected by that work.

My experiences outside the classroom have reinforced that perspective. Volunteering in community health settings has shown me how much communication, access, and trust matter alongside technical knowledge. Service has also taught me to value reliability, patience, and the ability to explain complicated ideas clearly. Those lessons shape the way I approach academic projects as well as the kind of work I hope to pursue in the future.

How to use this portfolio

This Day 4 ePortfolio brings together five pieces: this introduction, my resume, a personal statement, one selected artifact with a contextual statement, and a short reflection. Together, they provide a concise picture of my experience, interests, skills, and the direction in which I hope to continue developing.
