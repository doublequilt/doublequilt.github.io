Glucose–insulin dynamics

A quantitative project exploring how a mathematical model can represent a biological system.

Contextual statement

Why I selected this work

I chose this project because it represents the kind of interdisciplinary problem that interests me most. It required me to connect mathematics with biology, interpret what a model can and cannot explain, and communicate technical reasoning clearly. More importantly, it helped me see quantitative modeling not as an abstract exercise, but as a way to ask better questions about real biological processes. The project reflects my interest in combining analytical problem-solving with health-related questions and shows my willingness to work through uncertainty rather than treating a model as a perfect representation of reality.
